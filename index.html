How I Think

Choose Your Hard

Six principles I use to get anyone, at any age, actually using AI. This is the operating system, not a list of apps.

01

Start with the word

Reframe "artificial." It is not fake and it is not your replacement. It is a tool, and the fear lives mostly in the name.

02

Meet people where they stand

No assumed background. We start from what you already do every day and build the AI onto that, not the other way around.

03

Common sense is the superpower

Your judgment is the rare part. The machine is fast, you are wise. The combination beats either one alone.

04

Treat it like it's always loaded

Respect the tool. Verify, do not blindly trust, and never hand it the wheel on things that matter without watching.

05

Augment, never atrophy

Use it to get stronger, not softer. The goal is leverage on your skills, not a crutch that replaces them.

06

It is the worst it will ever be

Whatever it does today, it does better tomorrow. That is the case for starting now instead of waiting it out.
